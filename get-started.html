Getting started

Your computer.
Your team’s workspace.

Grants for Good is currently shared directly with invited beta teams. There is no public account signup or hosted workspace on this website.

Get the right installation.

Ask the person who invited you for the app package and the team’s workspace folder link. A preconfigured installation includes the Google desktop app configuration; it must never include someone else’s sign-in tokens or client records.

Windows

Use the supplied 64-bit Windows package, extract the ZIP, and double-click Grants for Good.bat. The full Windows package includes Python. Read the package’s START HERE instructions.

Linux

Use Python 3.10 or newer. Open the supplied app folder and use its Linux launcher, or run python3 app.py. Keep the app running while you work in your browser.

These pages do not distribute the private application source or a preconfigured team installer. Use the package supplied by your workspace owner.

Join once, with your own Google account.

  1. Open the app. It starts a local web address in your browser. That address belongs to your computer, not this public website.
  2. Confirm the workspace folder. Use the Google Drive folder shared by your team. An invited installer may already have it filled in.
  3. Sign in with your own Google account. The account needs edit access to the workspace folder. Read the Drive permission explanation before consenting.
  4. Check sharing. Post a harmless test message and ask a colleague to reply. Confirm that both computers receive it before relying on the workspace.

Your workspace administrator may need to add your Google account to the beta’s permitted users. If Google refuses the saved sign-in, use the app’s sign-in-again option.

Try it without client data.

The website’s interactive product illustration is safe to explore immediately. It uses fictional funders and does not connect to any account.

For the full installed app, the supplied source installation supports a separate fictional demo: from its app folder, run python3 app.py --demo --port 8766. Do not use real client data in a demo workspace.

Keep both copies current.

Use the app’s update instructions and packages supplied by the workspace owner. Close the app before replacing its files. Update both teammates’ installations before using newly shared record types.

If your local app is already running, the launcher offers Open, Restart, or Cancel. On Linux, python3 app.py --restart restarts the verified copy. Refresh your browser after an update.

Keep backups of the local workspace and shared records. Uninstalling the app, deleting a workspace, and revoking Google access are different actions; see your data controls.

Current beta: v0.135.2.

As of November 18, 2026, v0.135.2 restores the sign-in-again action when Google rejects an existing session. The preceding release added visual research reports, linked funder profiles, and report-and-spreadsheet packages.

Beta status means real-world validation is still in progress. This website’s publication is not a 1.0 release and does not change your Google consent settings or your installed copy.
