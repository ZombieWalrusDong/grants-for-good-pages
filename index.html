For grant researchers, writers, and small nonprofit teams who need to know what’s been found, what’s been decided, and what happens next.

Meet the workspace
  • Client knowledge comes first.

    Keep the organization, program, budget need, contacts, notes, and unanswered questions connected.

  • Evidence stays in view.

    Separate a useful lead from a verified rule. Keep the source and the date with the finding.

  • The team stays in control.

    Research suggestions, human review, and authorization to act are separate decisions.
