About the private beta.

Grants for Good is a software project by Matt Cornett for grant research, client context, and project work. The installed app is currently supplied directly to invited users. Publishing this website does not create a public hosted application, a paid service, or a promise of general availability.

Use an installation supplied or authorized by your workspace owner. These pages do not publish the private source code or grant rights to redistribute private client information, account configuration, or team installers.

Use the workspace responsibly.

Use GFG only for work you are authorized to do. Use your own connected accounts, respect your organization’s confidentiality rules, and do not attempt to access someone else’s records or bypass an account or approval restriction.

You are responsible for protecting your computer, checking who has access to the shared folder, and maintaining appropriate backups. Do not place confidential client information in public bug reports.

Research supports a decision; it does not make one.

Funder listings, public filings, imported findings, scores, and suggestions can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Confirm important information against current funder guidance before relying on it. Scores are not a probability of receiving a grant, and a high partial score is not a verified recommendation.

Human review, authorization to act, client approval, actual submission, and funder decisions are distinct events. Importing research does not authorize outreach, an application, or changes to a connected account. No funding outcome is guaranteed.

Your information and shared copies.

GFG does not claim ownership of your client records, original notes, or other information you place in the workspace. You must have the right to use and share that information and any third-party materials, including books, reports, and grant databases.

Exports can contain sensitive details. Only send them to intended recipients. Workspace history and shared copies may remain on multiple computers or in backups; coordinate retention and deletion with the workspace owner. See the privacy policy.

Google, GitHub, Instrumentl, funder websites, chat services, and other providers operate independently. Their availability, permissions, subscriptions, and terms still apply. This project is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google, GitHub, or Instrumentl.

Only use a connection in ways permitted by the relevant account owner and provider. Do not assume that app approval overrides a provider’s access rules.

The beta is provided as available, with no promise of uninterrupted operation, complete accuracy, compatibility with every computer, or fitness for a particular purpose. It may contain defects. Keep backups, verify important changes, and use an appropriate review process for consequential work.
