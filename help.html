The common questions.

Is this where I sign in to my workspace?

No. Open the installed app on your computer, then sign in there with your own Google account. This website explains the product; it does not host your client records.

Do I need Google Drive for the product tour?

No. The tour uses fictional examples in your browser. A shared, installed team workspace uses Google Drive and requires your own sign-in.

Why does Google ask for broad Drive access?

The current beta requests full Google Drive permission to support automatic discovery of team data files. Its workspace features are designed around the chosen team folder, but the Google grant is broader than that folder. See the full permission explanation.

Does a score mean we should apply?

No. Scores are suggestions supported by varying amounts of evidence. Read coverage, confidence, reasons, eligibility, and timing. Human review and authorization to act are separate.

Why are some factors unknown?

There is not enough evidence to rate them responsibly. The app reweights the known factors and shows coverage. Unknown does not mean average, and it should come with a concrete discovery step.

What happens to a good lead when the round is closed?

It can remain a future-cycle prospect. Record the closed round, the next source check, and any published future dates. Do not treat an old deadline as a confirmed new one.

Can I use the app offline?

The installed app saves work locally. Sharing needs an internet connection and the app running; pending work syncs when the connection is restored. Keep backups and check for conflicts after reconnecting.

Can a colleague open an exported report without the app?

Yes. Extract the report package and keep the HTML and spreadsheet together. Saved funder profiles travel inside the HTML report. Source websites still need internet access. Share the report only with its intended recipients.

Why am I being asked to sign in again?

A saved Google authorization may have expired or been revoked. Open the app’s Google connection and sign in again. A workspace administrator may also need to check the beta account list. Do not send anyone your password or authorization tokens.

Will this replace every part of Instrumentl?

GFG focuses on shared client context, research, review, project work, and handoffs. Instrumentl remains a separate service, with its own subscription, permissions, and account rules. Connections and supported actions depend on the team’s configuration.

Help us reproduce it.

For an app bug, use the in-app feedback tool when available. You can also report a public, non-sensitive bug on this website’s GitHub repository.

  • What were you trying to do?
  • What happened, and what did you expect?
  • Which app version and operating system are you using?
  • Can you reproduce it using a fictional example?
Public issues are public.

Leave out client names, project records, screenshots containing confidential information, folder links, account details, credentials, and sign-in tokens. Share sensitive details privately with your workspace owner.

For a private or account question.

Contact the workspace owner who supplied your installation through your existing private contact channel. They manage access to the shared folder and can coordinate account or record-removal requests.

Project developer: Matt Cornett on GitHub. Do not post private client information in a public issue. See the privacy policy for the app’s data handling and controls.
