About this policy.

Grants for Good (GFG) is a private beta software project by Matt Cornett for grant research and team project work. Each user runs a local application. This public website provides product information and support; it does not host a team’s working records.

Your workspace owner decides who is invited, what client information belongs in the workspace, and how team records are shared. This policy describes the app’s handling of information, not the privacy practices of every organization using it.

Information in the installed app.

The app stores information you enter or import: client and program profiles, contacts, notes, tasks, discussions, research findings, source links, grant preparation records, activity, revision history, and related workspace settings.

Workspace records are held locally on your computer. When sharing is connected, records also sync to the Google Drive workspace folder. Private sign-in settings and Google authorization tokens are stored in the current user’s local application settings, outside the shared workspace. Tokens are not included in ordinary client reports or source/update packages.

The beta uses local user-profile file protection; it does not currently encrypt these tokens in an operating-system keychain. Protect the computer and the user account that runs the app.

Google sign-in and Drive access.

Google sign-in allows the app to connect to Drive under your own account. The app receives an authorization token and account identity information, including the account name and email needed to identify the workspace participant.

The current permission is full Google Drive access.

The beta requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. This Google permission can allow viewing and managing files throughout the authorized Drive. It is broader than one selected folder and is not the narrower drive.file permission.

The app’s workspace features use this permission to check access to the folder you choose; create, read, and update GFG team data files; discover other team members’ data files; and transfer workspace resources or updates when those features are used. The app is designed to keep workspace operations within the configured folder, but that application behavior does not narrow the permission granted by Google.

Workspace activity is shared with people who can access the team’s folder. Google Drive link-sharing settings can allow access beyond named team members, so choose those settings carefully. The public product website does not receive your Google authorization tokens or operate the Drive connection.

Google account data is used for the connected app features described here, not sold or used for advertising. The app does not send Google account data to an unattended language-model job. If you choose to export content and submit it to a separate research service, that is a separate disclosure you control.

You can disconnect in the app and revoke access through Google Account connections. Google’s own handling of information is described in its privacy policy.

Research, exports, and other services.

The app may contact funder websites, public filing sources, and connected services when you request research updates or use configured integrations. Those services receive the requests needed for the feature and have their own terms and privacy practices.

Optional open-chat research involves exporting or opening a prepared packet and choosing to submit it to your chat service. Review the packet before sharing it. GFG does not run an unattended model job just because you use this website or open a workspace.

Instrumentl connections are optional and configured separately. Supported reads and approved actions involve that service under the connected account. A GFG research import is not permission to write to an external account.

Reports and spreadsheets can contain client-specific information and sources. Once you export or send them, copies may exist outside the app’s control. Share them only with intended recipients and follow your organization’s confidentiality rules.

The public website.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not set advertising cookies, use a third-party analytics script, create user accounts, or collect form submissions. Its fictional product tour runs in the browser and does not access workspace data.

GitHub may process standard hosting information, such as an IP address and request details. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following links to another website is subject to that website’s practices.

Bug reports posted to the public GitHub issue tracker are public. Do not include confidential information in them.

Disconnecting, deleting, and retaining records.

  • Sign out in the app to remove that installation’s saved Google sign-in tokens and profile. Local workspace records are retained. Signing out does not revoke Google’s grant.
  • Revoke Google access in your Google Account to stop the app using that authorization. This does not delete local records or existing Drive files.
  • Ask the workspace owner to remove team access if you leave the team. Existing exported copies and local caches need separate handling.
  • Coordinate deletion with the workspace owner. The app retains revision history, shared records, and backups. Deleting or uninstalling application files alone may leave user settings, workspace caches, Drive files, and other teammates’ copies behind.

There is no central public GFG account database to delete. Retention and removal of team records must account for shared copies, backups, and any legal or contractual obligations of the organization using the workspace.

Questions and changes.

For a privacy or access request, contact the person who supplied your workspace installation through your existing private channel. Developer: Matt Cornett. For non-sensitive product questions, use the support page.

When this policy changes, its date will be updated here. Material changes should also be brought to beta users’ attention with the relevant app update.
